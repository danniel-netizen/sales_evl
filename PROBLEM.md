Business Problem:
________________

A retail shop wants to understand its sales, product returns, and repeat customers.

The shop has historical transaction data containing:

Customer details

Product/item details

Purchase date

Quantity

Selling price

Payment method

Return information

Customer purchase history


The business wants to use this data to answer:
______________________________________________

Which products are selling the most?

Which products are being returned frequently?

Which customers are repeat customers?

How frequently does a customer return to the shop?

What is the total sales amount after accounting for returns?

Which customers/products generate the most revenue?

Can we predict whether a customer is likely to make another purchase?

Can we identify products that have a higher probability of being returned?
